# Asset provenance

The Polaris star and favicon are from the Polaris project. Provider marks are copied from `frontend/assets/providers/` in [Polaris v1.0.0](https://github.com/nguywnben/polaris/tree/v1.0.0/frontend/assets/providers). `release.js` lists the 23 displayed providers. Provider names and marks identify their respective brands.

The landing page console is an illustrative, localized HTML preview. It does not display production screenshots, credentials, traffic statistics or real requests. The approved sidebar, routing diagram, sample credential rows and trace timeline preserve the earlier landing page style.

Provider image pixels are unchanged. Local PNG metadata records their source. No generative image editing was used. The inherited project license is included in `LICENSE`.

Social banner assets have separate attribution in `../social/README.md`.

The routing schematic shares the banner's OpenAI, Anthropic and Google SVGs from `../social/assets/`. These identify the three brands; provider catalog entries continue to use the corresponding Polaris adapter marks. OpenAI and Anthropic invert in dark mode, while Google's colors are preserved.
